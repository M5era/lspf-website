Rooted in Lisbon. Open to the world.

The second edition of Lisbon Street Photo Fest brings together photographers, enthusiasts, and curious minds from around the world for three days at Faculdade de Belas-Artes.

2026 Open Call — Submit your work →

An edition, an annual.

From 25–27 September 2026, we will gather in Lisbon for the second edition of Lisbon Street Photo Fest — a celebration of street photography across cultures, continents and emotions. Three days of exhibitions, talks, workshops and walks, set in the city's oldest neighborhoods and its most generous light.
