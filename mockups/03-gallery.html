i.The festival

An annual gathering of street photographers in Lisbon.

From 25–27 September 2026, we will meet in Lisbon for the second edition of Lisbon Street Photo Fest. A celebration of street photography that brings together photographers, enthusiasts and curious minds from around the world for three days at Faculdade de Belas-Artes — exhibitions, talks, workshops and walks across the city.
