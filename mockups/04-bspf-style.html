Join us for the second edition.

From 25–27 September 2026, we will meet in Lisbon for the second edition of Lisbon Street Photo Fest — a celebration of street photography that brings together photographers, enthusiasts and curious minds from around the world. Exhibitions, talks, workshops and walks across three days at Faculdade de Belas-Artes.
